Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains how Frenchefy handles information when you use the French-study journal at frenchefy.netlify.app. Frenchefy is developed by Ajay Kumar Reddy (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

Information you choose to store

Frenchefy lets you record study dates and durations, set a daily study goal, and choose a light or dark theme.

  • Without sign-in: these records and preferences are saved in local storage in your browser. They are not sent to our cloud database by the app unless you choose to sign in and cloud sync is configured.
  • With Google sign-in and cloud sync: your study dates, durations, daily goal, and theme are synced to a Supabase project. Supabase also processes account identifiers and the email address made available through Google sign-in to authenticate your account.

Do not enter sensitive personal, financial, health, or other confidential information in your study records.

How information is used

Information is used to provide the journal, calculate your progress, remember your preferences, authenticate your account, and synchronize your records when cloud sync is enabled. We do not use study records for advertising, and Frenchefy does not include an analytics or advertising tracker.

Service providers and external links

The website is hosted by Netlify. Optional account authentication and cloud storage are provided by Supabase and Google. If you follow the voluntary support link, Ko-fi handles that visit under its own privacy policy. These providers may process technical information needed to operate their services. Review their policies for details about their practices; their services are governed by their own terms.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share study records with third parties except as needed to provide the services you request, comply with law, or protect the service and its users.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Browser-only data remains in your browser until you remove it or clear the site’s stored data. Cloud records remain in Supabase while the account is active. To request deletion of cloud data associated with your account, contact us using the address below. Deleting browser data does not delete cloud records, and deleting an account or cloud data does not automatically clear copies stored in other browsers. Provider backups may persist for a limited period under the provider’s retention practices.

Security and international processing

We use Supabase’s account-based access controls for cloud records, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to their applicable safeguards and terms.

Children

Frenchefy is a general-purpose study tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided account information so we can review the request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The “Last updated” date above indicates the latest revision. Continued use after an update means the revised policy applies to future use.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, email ajayreddy.tech@gmail.com.
